# STATUS — the hub

## Now

This is the hub folder's own state file, and the one place that says where
things stand here. The board and the tree page both read it, so keep it
truthful as you go rather than at the end: what is happening now, in a short
paragraph, written for the person who opens this folder next week and has
forgotten everything. Replace this paragraph with your own the first time you
work here.

## Important

- The schema is fixed, and every project's STATUS.md uses it: `## Now` is one
  short paragraph of where things stand · `## To do` is `- [ ]` items, a
  leading `!` inside the item marks it urgent, and a finished item is ticked
  (`- [x]`) rather than deleted · `## Important` is the constraints, rulings
  and decisions that must survive this file being rewritten.
