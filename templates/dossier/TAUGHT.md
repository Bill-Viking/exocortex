# Taught

Append-only. Each time you tell a session something to remember about how you
work or how you decide, it adds one dated line at the end of this file rather
than editing the other pages of your dossier in place. The other pages are
folded and reorganised from this log now and then; this one only accumulates,
in order, so the record of what was learned and when is never lost.

One line per lesson, oldest first:

`- YYYY-MM-DD: what was taught, in your own words.`

A line that states a RULE of behaviour carries a stamp saying what became of
it, in square brackets at the end — `[PROMOTED <date>]` when it was written
into your own standing rules, `[JOURNAL-ONLY]` when it stays here. A rule-shaped
line with no stamp is still waiting on your word, and the tools that read this
file will say so.
