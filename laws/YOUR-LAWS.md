# Your laws

This is a starter template. Your approved rules live in your own law book, `{{HUB}}/dossier/LAWS.md`: the installer writes it once as a two-line header, and the CLAUDE.md it writes loads it beside the engine's laws. Do not put personal facts in this shipped template or commit them to the public program.

Record a new preference in `dossier/TAUGHT.md` first. Ask the owner whether it should become a standing law or remain journal-only. On approval, `python3 tools/personal.py --promote` writes a short rule with its scope and reason to your law book and marks the journal entry as promoted. Update an existing rule instead of creating a conflicting copy.
